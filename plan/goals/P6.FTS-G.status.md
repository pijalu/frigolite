## Root-cause findings for 5.7 (investigated, NOT yet fixed)

- After the 5.3 merges, `%_segdir` has a PATHOLOGICAL btree: ~90 pages (deep
  interior levels with mostly-empty leaves) for 19 rows. The segdir's many
  small insert/delete cycles (flushes + crisis deletes) leave empty leaves and
  a deep tree.
- The auto-rowid allocator's `scanMaxRowID` (full cursor scan) can STOP before
  the true maximum in this tree — it missed the L2[3] row (rowid 1067) after
  5.3. The next segdir insert reused rowid 1067 → a DUPLICATE-rowid cell
  (level-0 and level-2 both at 1067).
- The crisis's `deleteFTSSegdirLevel(0)` (internal `DeleteStmt WHERE level=0`)
  then matched the level-0 cell at rowid 1067 and the delete removed BOTH
  cells at that rowid (duplicate-rowid btree cells) → L2[3] vanished.
- Evidence: the btree cursor via a FRESH pager visits all 19 cells (incl.
  1067); the LIVE pager's scan during the flush missed 1067; the DELETE's
  `collectDeleteRows` saw TWO cells at rowid 1067 (level 0 and level 2).
