(xConnect/xBestIndex/xFilter callbacks, `::vtab_constraints` variable capture,
`$hdl constraints`/`orderby`/`mask`/`distinct` subcommands) is TCL-proc
infrastructure that tcl2go cannot express. Per the coordinator's directive
these are the genuinely untranspilable parts; the ENGINE side of the contract
they exercise must be implemented and pinned by native tests (supplement, not
replacement, per .agents/lessons_learned.md MANDATORY RULES).
