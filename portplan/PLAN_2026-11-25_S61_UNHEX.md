## Risks

- Do not quote all numeric-looking Tcl values: other fixtures depend on
  canonical numeric inlining (`TestTclSQLLiteral`). Only non-canonical
  leading-zero spellings change.
- Unicode escape parsing must stop at the first non-hex byte; consuming four
  bytes blindly would turn `\uE01ABCD` into U+E01A.
- Compare raw fail sets only; no full-suite line greps.
